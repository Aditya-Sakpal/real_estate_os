# Production spec: drone construction progress update

Read `../INSTA-PRODUCTION-NOTES.md` first.

**Target length:** 10 to 15 seconds
**Crew:** drone pilot
**Site time:** 20 minutes per visit
**Post time:** 30 minutes

The lowest-ceiling and highest-trust format in the set. It will not go viral and it is
not supposed to. What it does is keep existing buyers calm, give channel partners
something to forward, and build a dated public record that your project is progressing.

For a developer under RERA, that public record has value well beyond marketing.

---

## Part A: the one thing that makes this format work

**Fly the exact same path every month.** Everything else is secondary.

A progress reel where each month is shot from a slightly different position is just a set
of drone shots. A progress reel where the framing is identical and only the building
changes is a time-lapse, and a time-lapse of a building going up is genuinely compelling
in a way that individual shots never are.

This means the first flight is the important one, because it sets the template for the
next thirty months.

### The flight log

Create this file on the first visit and fill it in every single time. Keep it in the
project folder, not in someone's phone.

```
PROJECT: Ashwin Residences, Kalyan West
FLIGHT TEMPLATE v1  (set 2026-09-24)

WAYPOINT A (the hero, static hold)
  Lat / Long:         19.2403 / 73.1305
  Altitude AGL:       55 m
  Heading:            212 deg
  Gimbal tilt:        -35 deg
  Hold duration:      12 s

WAYPOINT B (the rise)
  Start alt:          12 m    End alt: 60 m
  Heading:            212 deg (locked)
  Gimbal:             -20 deg to -55 deg
  Duration:           10 s

WAYPOINT C (the orbit)
  Centre lat/long:    19.2403 / 73.1305
  Radius:             45 m
  Altitude:           40 m
  Direction:          clockwise, 90 deg arc
  Duration:           12 s

VISIT LOG
  Date        Floors cast   Weather      Pilot   Notes
  2026-09-24  G+4           clear        RS      first flight, template set
```

If your drone supports saved waypoint missions, save this as a mission and fly it
automatically. Most DJI consumer drones do. That removes human variance entirely and it
is worth the twenty minutes of setup.

If it does not, fly manually to the logged numbers and check the telemetry overlay before
you start recording.

### Practical constraints
- **Same time of day, within an hour.** Shadow direction changing between months is the
  second most visible inconsistency after framing.
- **Same season is impossible, so plan for monsoon.** June to September in Maharashtra
  you will get grey flat light and some months you will not fly at all. Do not force a
  flight in bad weather, skip the month and note it in the log.
- Check Digital Sky airspace before every flight, not just the first.

---

## Part B: the shots

Three, per the flight log above. Shoot all three every visit even though a single reel
uses one or two, because in eighteen months you will want the material.

1. **Waypoint A, the static hold.** This is the frame that becomes the time-lapse. Guard
   it. 12 seconds, zero drift.
2. **Waypoint B, the rise.** Your opener.
3. **Waypoint C, the orbit.** Shows context and the surrounding development.

Plus one ground shot, handheld, 6 seconds: the site board with the project name and RERA
number, readable. Use it as the closing frame every month. It is small and it does a
surprising amount of compliance and credibility work.

---

## Part C: two ways to cut it

### C1. The monthly update (what you post every month)

15 seconds. Simple.

| Time | Shot | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 to 3.5s | Waypoint B, the rise | Project name, 48pt, upper third, from 0.4s |
| 3.5 to 9.0s | Waypoint A, static hold | Progress line at 4.0s: `September 2026 | 7th slab cast` |
| 9.0 to 12.5s | Waypoint C, the orbit | `On schedule for December 2027` |
| 12.5 to 15.0s | Site board, static | RERA lockup |

Keep it plain. This is not the format where you get clever. A buyer who has paid a
booking amount wants to see the building and the date, and anything else you add is in
their way.

### C2. The accumulating time-lapse (what you post every six months)

This is the payoff, and it is why the flight log matters.

- Take the Waypoint A static hold from every month you have.
- Stabilise and align all of them to the first one. If your framing discipline held, this
  takes minutes.
- Cut them in sequence, 0.8 seconds each, with a 6 frame dissolve between.
- Burn a date stamp in a fixed position, bottom left, changing on each cut.
- Twelve months gives you a 10 second reel of a building growing out of the ground.

At six months this is mildly interesting. At eighteen months it is the single best piece
of content your project will ever have, and you cannot make it retrospectively. That is
the argument for starting the log on the very next flight rather than when you get
around to it.

---

## Part D: text on screen

- Project name, 48pt, from 0.4s.
- **Progress line, 40pt, from 4.0s.** This is the substance. Be specific and be honest:
  `7th slab cast` beats `work progressing well`. `Plaster started, towers A and B` beats
  `interior work underway`.
- **Schedule line, 36pt.** Either `On schedule for December 2027` or, when it is not,
  say so. A month where you post `Two weeks behind on Tower B, revised internal target
  January 2028` will cost you nothing with existing buyers and will earn you more trust
  than six months of `on schedule`. They can see the site. They talk to each other.
- RERA lockup on the site board frame.

## Part E: caption

```
Ashwin Residences, September 2026.

7th slab cast. Plaster started on Tower A.
RERA possession: December 2027. On schedule.

MahaRERA P52100012345 | maharera.maharashtra.gov.in

Monthly updates here on the 25th. Booked buyers, the detailed report is on WhatsApp.

#kalyanwest #underconstruction #constructionupdate #reraproject
```

The line about a fixed posting date does real work. Buyers who know an update comes on
the 25th stop calling on the 20th.

---

## Part F: cadence and the actual reason to do this

Post on the same date every month per project. Put it in a calendar and do not skip, and
when you do skip because of monsoon, post a still with a line explaining why.

The engagement on these will be low and you should not care. What you are buying is:

1. **Fewer support calls.** Existing buyers check the feed instead of calling sales.
2. **Channel partner ammunition.** Brokers forward these to their own clients, which is
   distribution you do not have to pay for.
3. **A dated record.** If a possession dispute ever arises, a public monthly record of
   actual site progress is a materially better position than internal photographs nobody
   else has seen.
4. **The eighteen-month time-lapse**, per Part C2.

Of the seven formats in this library, this is the one with the lowest immediate payoff
and the highest probability that you will be glad you ran it.
