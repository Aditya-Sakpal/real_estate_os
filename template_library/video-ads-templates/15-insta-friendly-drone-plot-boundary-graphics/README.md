# Drone plot with boundary graphics

## The format

Aerial over land with an animated glowing outline tracing the plot boundary and the area figure counting up or stamping on.

## Why it works

Solves the hardest problem in plot and land sales: people cannot judge size from the air. The outline gives scale instantly. Very high comment rates because viewers ask about price and location in the comments rather than DMing.

## How to shoot it

- Fly the plot at two heights: one high establishing, one lower orbit.
- The outline should trace on, not appear. The drawing motion is what holds attention.
- Area figure in the local unit that buyers actually use. In India that is sq ft for flats and guntha, acre or sq ft for land depending on the market.
- One number on screen. Adding price, config and area at once kills it.
