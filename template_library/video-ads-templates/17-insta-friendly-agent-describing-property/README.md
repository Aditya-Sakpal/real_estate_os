# Agent describing the property to camera

## The format

A presenter stands at the property and talks to camera while large animated text lands
word by word in sync with what they are saying. The reel cuts between the presenter and
the property, and runs longer than most reels here: 45 to 70 seconds rather than 10.

## Why it works

It is the only format in this set that can carry real information. A land-to-home
transformation makes someone feel something for six seconds. This one can explain a
configuration, answer an objection and quote a price, and hold attention while it does
it, because the kinetic text gives the eye something to track even when the viewer has
the sound off.

The text is not decoration. On a muted scroll it is the entire reel. Both of the
presenter references run text through effectively every second of their runtime.

## How to shoot it

- Presenter at the property, outdoors, property visible behind them on both sides.
- Lav mic. The text is cut to the voice, so the voice has to be clean or the sync is
  guesswork.
- Shoot more coverage than you think: this runs a minute, and a minute of one angle is
  unwatchable.
- Write the text before you shoot. The script and the supers are the same document.

## Adapting it for an Indian residential developer

- This is a better fit than the trending-audio format this folder used to hold, because
  it does not depend on a sound you do not control and it survives being watched muted.
- It overlaps with 16 (presenter site price reveal). The difference: 16 is shot on a live
  construction site in PPE and is about credibility, this one is shot at a finished or
  sample property and is about the product. Run 16 for under-construction inventory and
  17 for ready or sample flats.
- Hindi or Marathi VO with the kinetic text in English works well here, because the text
  is carrying the specs and the voice is carrying the persuasion.
