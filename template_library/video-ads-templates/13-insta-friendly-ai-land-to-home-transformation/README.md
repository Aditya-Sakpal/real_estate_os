# AI land-to-home transformation

## The format

An empty plot, field or bare site morphs into the finished building in one continuous shot. The camera barely moves; the world changes around it.

## Why it works

This is the single most-shared format in the set and the most Indian-native. It answers the one question every plot buyer has (what will actually stand here?) in about six seconds, with zero technical literacy required. Shares outrun likes because people send it to family who are deciding together.

## How to shoot it

- Shoot the plot as-is first: one locked-off or slow-push shot, mid-morning, no people in frame.
- Keep the camera move minimal. The transformation is the motion; a moving camera fights it.
- Chalk or lime the foundation footprint on the ground before shooting. Every strong example in this set does it, and it makes the AI transition read as grounded rather than pasted.
- End on the finished elevation held for 2 seconds so it can be screenshotted.
- Caption carries the specs (plot area, config, location). The video carries the feeling.

## Adapting it for an Indian residential developer

- This maps directly to your 02-launch-teaser and 10-construction-update categories.
- For a MahaRERA-registered project this is where the QR lockup belongs on the final held frame, not over the transition.
- Do not imply the render is a photograph of a completed building. Label it as an artist impression, which is also the RERA-safe position.
