# Cinematic slow tour

## The format

Restrained, moody camera through the site and interiors. No hard sell, no supers beyond one line of copy.

## Why it works

The opposite bet to everything else in this set: it wins on saves rather than shares. Positions the developer as a brand rather than a seller, which matters for premium inventory where price-led content cheapens the product.

## How to shoot it

- Golden hour only. This format dies in flat light.
- One camera move per shot. Let shots run 3 to 4 seconds.
- The 'move your thumb' opener is a genuine trick worth stealing: it reframes scrolling as participation.
- Ambient sound or a single sustained track. No voiceover.
