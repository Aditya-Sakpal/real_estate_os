# Real estate ad template library, video

35 reference reels across 7 categories (00 to 12, some numbers retired), each an MP4 with its sound plus a
`__poster.jpg` still taken a third of the way in. Replaced on 26 September 2026: the earlier
Canva previews were generic and most had no sound, so each category now holds real Indian
real estate reels found through Google's short-video search and saved with indown.io.
A reel with no sound, or near silence, was left out.

`00-narrated-story` holds the reference films for the studio's **Narrated story** choice:
story-led property ads with a voice over and music. It is shown on that choice's own tile,
never as a design template.

These are other people's ads, kept as reference for study. They are never reposted or cut
into a client's reel.

## Also in this folder: reel formats 13 to 19

Six Instagram reel formats (folders `13-insta-friendly-*` to `19-insta-friendly-*`).
These are not Canva templates. Each holds a breakdown of the format, a `PRODUCTION-SPEC.md`
(shot list, on-site prep, frame-accurate beat timing, text and lockup placement, caption),
and the reference reels themselves as MP4s.

Start with `INSTA-PRODUCTION-NOTES.md` (safe areas, price and MahaRERA lockup geometry,
disclaimer wording, export settings) and `INSTA-REEL-INDEX.md` (the seven ranked, and a
suggested order of work). `REELS-ON-DISK.md` inside each folder maps every video file
there back to its source post and the note on why it was picked.

| Folder | Format | Reels on disk | Pairs with category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | AI land-to-home transformation | 4 | 06-construction-update |
| 14 | VFX dust transition reveal | 3 | 01-launch-film |
| 17 | Agent describing the property to camera | 4 | none |
| 19 | Construction progress | 9 | 06-construction-update |

The reels in 01 to 12 show the edit, the type and the lockup; these specs give you the
footage to put under them.

`INSTA-HOW-TO-DOWNLOAD.md` covers pulling down the reels not yet on disk, and how they
may and may not be used.
