# Skill: building assembly, in one generation

The folder keeps its old name; the template is **Building assembly** in the
studio. This is what the product knows about the format and how it writes one
film in a single Seedance 2.5 call. The code version is `DUST_REVEAL_SKILL` in
`src/lib/video-skills.ts` (key unchanged); change both together.

Rewritten on 5 October 2026. The first rewrite that day made it a dust cloud
with the finished tower inside it, and the client called the film "absolutely
pathetic": every reel here builds the building, piece by piece.

## Verdict

**MAKE**, from one approved exterior render; `needs` is null. The ground
before is the approved picture's own setting with the building's spot bare.
Every piece is part of the approved building, in its own shape: the same
footprint, floors, towers and facade. The pieces move by themselves: no
workers, cranes or a building site. The finished building is the approved
picture exactly.

## What the product does with it

**15 seconds, one continuous take, 4 to 5 moments of 2 to 4 seconds, nobody
speaks, 1 to 2 cards, white text** (`oneTake`, `assembles`, `cardAccent`).

1. **The ground, about 2 s.** Bare earth where the building will stand, the
   camera low and drifting toward it. A hook card from `hooks`: "WATCH IT
   RISE", "WAIT FOR IT" or "WATCH THIS".
2. **The frame, about 4 s.** Footings and columns rise out of the earth, beams
   lock, the frame climbs to full height.
3. **The skin, about 3.5 s.** Slabs stack, facade panels, windows and balcony
   glass snap on from bottom to top, the camera rising with them.
4. **The finish, about 3 s.** The last panels lock, the landscaping rolls out,
   the dust settles; the building is the approved picture. A soft hit.
5. **The close, about 2.5 s.** Held, with the price card (ours) or "BOOK A
   SITE VISIT". With four moments, the finish and the close are one.

The checks know the format: the building is judged only in the last two
moments, and the sweep reads a half-built frame as the project, not as
another building.

Forbidden: a cut or a second place; workers, cranes or scaffolding; a frame
or facade in any other shape, or one floor more; an amenity or a room;
anybody on screen; before-and-after captions; speech; coloured text; a place
name, the project's name or a number on a card the model draws.
