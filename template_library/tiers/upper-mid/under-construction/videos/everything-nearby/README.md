# Everything nearby (upper-mid, under construction)

A 35 to 50 second reel that sells the location: the station, the mall, the hospital,
the school, each with its distance, then the project.

## What the references do
- Hook in the first 2 seconds: "Premium home, premium location" or a question.
- The project's name over the site or sales office, then a drone or map shot with a pin.
- One nearby place per beat, its name or logo and the minutes away.
- Close on the project name and one line ("Premium homes, well-connected location").
- The broker version adds a satellite map, a highlighted plot, an upcoming road, the
  price and a WhatsApp button, with the RERA number on every frame.

Each video has `__poster.jpg` (its cover) and `__frames.jpg` (24 frames, read left to
right, to see the structure without playing it).
