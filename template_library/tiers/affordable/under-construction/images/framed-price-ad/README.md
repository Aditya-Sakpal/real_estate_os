# Framed price ad (affordable, under construction)

The affordable tier's main ad: one picture that carries the price, the
configuration, the locality, the RERA block and the phone number at once.

## What every reference does
- Top band: developer logo left; MahaRERA number, QR and the Authority's website right.
- One big lifestyle picture: a family on a balcony, a person at an amenity, or a person
  beside the tower. AI people, labelled "Image for representation purpose only".
- Headline in 3 to 7 words. Kumar sets it in an italic serif; Paradise in heavy
  two-colour caps on a placard the person holds.
- Price in a box: configuration, "STARTING", the amount in Lakh, star, "+TAXES" or
  "ONWARDS". Never in the headline.
- Project name and a location pin line near the price.
- A coloured contact bar at the foot: phone and website.
